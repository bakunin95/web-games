Design pillars (must not break)

  • Readable in one glance — threat, path, and goal visible from spawn
  • One new idea per room — then remix, never pile-on
  • Construction fantasy — girders, ladders, switches, conveyors as verbs
  • Fair fail — deaths teach; no hidden crush windows
  • Short loop — enter → solve → exit in under ~90s for early rooms
